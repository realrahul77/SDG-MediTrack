About This Project

Web Development Final Project — SDG 3: Good Health & Well-being

🌍 UN SDG Goal 3

MediTrack — Medication & Health Tracker

A web application designed to help individuals manage their medications, track health vitals, and build consistent health routines — contributing to SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being.

Selected SDG

SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being

This goal focuses on ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all ages. It targets reducing preventable deaths, fighting diseases, and promoting mental and physical health globally. Medication non-adherence is one of the leading causes of hospital re-admissions and preventable complications worldwide.

Problem Statement

Millions of patients — especially elderly individuals and those with chronic conditions — regularly forget to take their medications or take incorrect doses. In India alone, medication non-adherence leads to worsening of conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and tuberculosis, placing a massive burden on the healthcare system and individuals' quality of life.

Proposed Solution

MediTrack is a browser-based medication and health tracking web app. Users can add their prescriptions, set reminder preferences, mark doses as taken or missed, and log health vitals like blood pressure and heart rate — all in a clean, intuitive interface with no backend required.

Who Will Use It

  • Elderly patients managing multiple medications
  • Chronic disease patients (diabetes, hypertension)
  • Caregivers managing medications for family members
  • Health-conscious individuals tracking their wellness
  • Patients following post-surgical medication schedules
⚙️ Key Features
💊 Medication Management

Add, view, and delete medications with dosage, schedule, frequency, and instructions. Full CRUD support in-browser.

📅 Daily Schedule Tracker

View all doses grouped by time of day. Mark each dose as taken or missed with a single click.

⏰ Reminder Settings

Toggle morning, afternoon, evening, and night reminders independently to match personal routines.

🩺 Health Log

Log vitals including blood pressure, weight, heart rate, and symptom notes with date tracking.

📊 Adherence Dashboard

Real-time stats showing total medications, today's doses, adherence percentage, and missed doses.

💾 Local Data Persistence

All data is saved to localStorage, so your medications and logs persist across browser sessions with no server needed.

🚀 Future Scope
  • Push Notifications: Browser-based push notifications to remind users at the exact scheduled dose time, even when the app is not open.
  • Mobile App: A React Native mobile application to give users a native experience on Android and iOS with alarm integration.
  • Caregiver Mode: Allow family members or caregivers to monitor and update a patient's medication schedule remotely via a shared link.
  • Doctor Reports: Auto-generate PDF medication adherence reports to share with doctors during consultations.
  • Drug Interaction Checker: Integrate an API to warn users about potential interactions between their listed medications.
  • Multi-language Support: Hindi and regional language support to serve a broader Indian population, especially in rural areas.
