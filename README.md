# SDG-Meditrack
1. Selected SDG and Reason for Selection
SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being: This goal focuses on ensuring healthy lives and promoting
well-being for all ages. It targets reducing preventable deaths, fighting diseases, and promoting mental
and physical health globally.

# Why this issue is important:
Medication non-adherence is one of the leading causes of hospital re-
admissions and preventable complications worldwide. Assisting individuals in adhering to their medical
prescriptions directly contributes to improved global health outcomes.
